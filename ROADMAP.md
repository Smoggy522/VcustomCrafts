# Roadmap

The source in this repository implements the stable custom crafting core. Planned modules from the larger product specification are intentionally separated so released descriptions remain accurate.

## 1.1

- furnace, blast furnace, smoker and campfire recipes;
- stonecutter and modern smithing recipes;
- recipe pagination, search and categories;
- dedicated ItemsAdder, Oraxen and Nexo provider modules after validation against their current official APIs.

## 1.2

- SQLite player data;
- unlocks, favorites, cooldowns and per-player limits;
- Vault economy and PlaceholderAPI requirements;
- crafting statistics and public Bukkit events.

## 2.0

- configurable custom stations;
- timed crafting and queues;
- particles, sounds and progress UI;
- MySQL/MariaDB storage;
- recipe packs and import/export tooling.
